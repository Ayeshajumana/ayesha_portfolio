Process Control

LASER PARAMETERS

ENERGY OUTPUT

POWER

Energy delivered by the laser. High power yields stronger material interaction.

HEAD VELOCITY

SPEED

Movement speed of the laser head. Low speed delivers more energy per unit length.

OPTICAL WAIST

FOCUS

Concentration of the beam. Optimum focal point ensures minimum kerf width.

CYCLE COUNT

PASSES

Number of times the laser follows the path, preventing scorching on dense materials.

“Correct parameters depend on the laser system, material, thickness and desired result.”

Laboratory Protocol

LASER SAFETY

⚠
LASER HAZARD
CLASS 4 ENCLOSED OPERATION
  • ✓ PROTECTIVE ENCLOSURE: Interlocked casing prevents laser beam exposure.
  • ✓ CONTROLLED OPERATION: Calibrated laser settings to prevent workpiece flare-up.
  • ✓ APPROPRIATE VENTILATION: Forced fume extraction for smoke and particulates.
  • ✓ MATERIAL COMPATIBILITY: Never cut hazardous materials (e.g., PVC/chlorine).
  • ✓ SUPERVISED OPERATION: Active technician presence throughout processing.

“Laser systems should be operated according to the machine manufacturer's safety requirements and appropriate laboratory procedures.”
