Operational Control

LASER PARAMETERS

ENERGY FLUX

POWER

High power delivers stronger energy for deep cuts; tuned power avoids burning on scans.

FEED RATE

SPEED

Low speed increases dwell time and energy per unit length; high speed enables light surface rastering.

OPTICAL SPOT

FOCUS

Correct focal distance achieves smallest spot diameter and narrowest kerf width.

CYCLES

PASSES

Multiple passes reduce edge scorching when processing thicker composite materials.

“Correct parameters depend on the laser system, material, thickness and desired result.”

MATERIAL DEPENDENCY

Laser performance depends on material absorption properties.

THICKNESS

Maximum thickness depends on machine capability and optics.

HEAT EFFECT

Localized heat can cause slight thermal discoloration on edges.

KERF

Material width removed along the laser path must be compensated.

FOCUS

Incorrect focal distance reduces power density and cut sharpness.

PARAMETERS

Speed and power require calibrated tuning per substrate.

LASER SAFETY

⚠
LASER RADIATION
CLASS 4 ENCLOSED SYSTEM
  • ✓ PROTECTIVE ENCLOSURE: Interlocked shielding during active beam operation.
  • ✓ APPROPRIATE VENTILATION: Active exhaust extraction for fumes and particulate.
  • ✓ MATERIAL COMPATIBILITY: Never process hazardous substrates (e.g. PVC, vinyl).
  • ✓ SUPERVISED OPERATION: Active monitoring to prevent material flare-up.

“Laser systems should be operated according to the machine manufacturer's safety requirements and appropriate laboratory procedures.”
