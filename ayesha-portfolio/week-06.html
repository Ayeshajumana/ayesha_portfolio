Technology Classification

TYPES OF 3D PRINTING TECHNOLOGIES

The primary digital fabrication mechanisms across polymer, photopolymer, powder, and metallic systems.

DIGITAL FABRICATION TAXONOMY FDM/FFF • SLA • SLS • SLM/DMLS • DED
01 THERMOPLASTIC FILAMENT

FDM / FFF

“Melted filament is deposited layer by layer.”

02 PHOTOPOLYMER RESIN

SLA

“Liquid resin is selectively cured using light.”

03 POLYMER POWDER

SLS

“A laser selectively sinters powdered material.”

04 METAL POWDER

SLM / DMLS

“Metal powder is selectively fused using a high-energy source.”

05 METAL POWDER / WIRE

DED

“Material is deposited directly onto a component.”

WHY STL?

“STL represents the surface geometry of a 3D object as a mesh of triangles.”

SMOOTH MODEL → TRIANGULAR FACETS → STL

“STL is commonly used to represent 3D surface geometry in digital fabrication workflows.”
